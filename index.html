What we're trying to learn

We're asking practitioners three open questions. Pick whichever you have a strong feeling about. Even one short answer is hugely useful.

1

Where do the hours go?

When you take on a new IRS collection client, where do the most hours actually go before you can submit the case? What part of the workflow eats time without adding value?

2

What's almost-right about your tools?

What software do you use today for collection cases, and where does it fall short or leave you doing manual work you wish it handled?

3

What would you automate?

If you could wave a wand and have one specific workflow magically automated tomorrow, what would it be?

Who we're talking to

EAs, CPAs, and tax attorneys running solo or 2 to 10 person firms where IRS representation is the main service: Offer in Compromise, Installment Agreement, Currently Not Collectible, penalty abatement, audit defense. Especially anyone who's thought about hiring a paralegal just to handle Form 433 intake.
